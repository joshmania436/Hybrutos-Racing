Sub-teams

Powertrain

The heart of our car. Runs a hybrid powertrain. Including a RC200 engine and an Agni 119R motor. Coupled by our very own patented coupler.

Chassis and suspension

The skeleton and the muscle. Our chassis is a compact tubular chassis which helps house all the systems to run the car including the driver. The suspension system is what decides how our car reacts to turning and accelerating.

Electronics

The electronics of the car make split second decisions from taking in input from the driver to giving output to the wheels to saving a life in case of any catastrophic failure. In short, electronics is the brain of the car and fuelling it all is a big hybrid battery!

Aero and design

The package that brings grip and efficiency in style. The Aerodynamic components help add more downforce and grip to the car while also reducing drag and increasing efficiency of the car.

Business and media

“Sell me this car” and outreach under one roof. The business models are born here, talking numbers and graphs. Media is what makes us known to the world, from social media posts to website design.
